# Known issues / intentional limitations

- The v1.0.0 release target and final hardware-validation platform is **New
  Nintendo 3DS**. Old Nintendo 3DS performance/support is not claimed.
- Pause/PDA touchscreen cursor placement is tuned for the lower-screen scaled
  presentation and may not land on the exact physical pixel under the stylus;
  the accepted v1.0.0 mapping remains usable for menu selection.
- Touch is intentionally limited to frontend/pause/PDA UI. Gameplay uses the
  physical 3DS controls.
